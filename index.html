StitchKit

A private Windows desktop app for preparing and listing digital cross stitch patterns.

StitchKit is built and used by one seller to run their own shop of printable cross stitch patterns. It is not offered to other sellers, and there is nothing to sign up for or download here.

What it does

  • Turns a cross stitch chart into a ready listing kit: US Letter and A4 PDF patterns, listing images with alt text, and a short video.
  • Writes the listing's title, description and tags on the seller's computer.
  • Creates each new listing as a draft digital-download listing in the seller's own shop, with its images, files and video. The seller reviews and publishes every draft by hand.
  • Updates the seller's own listings when their wording, price, images or files change.

On Pinterest

  • Creates pins on the seller's own Pinterest account, on boards the seller chooses. Each pin has an image made for it, a title, a description, alt text and a link to the matching listing in the seller's shop.
  • Posts only what the seller plans or approves in the app.
  • Doesn't read other people's pins or profiles, and never follows, likes or comments.

Privacy

  • The app runs only on the seller's own computer. There is no server and no account.
  • It connects to the seller's shop through the official API, signing in with OAuth 2.0 (PKCE). The sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on that computer and are never sent anywhere else.
  • It doesn't collect, sell or share anyone's data, and it never stores buyers' names or addresses.
  • It doesn't scrape websites or access other shops.
